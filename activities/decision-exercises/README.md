# Decision Exercises

Decision exercises ask learners to choose a course of action and defend the reasoning.

## Common Formats

- Proceed, modify, pause, or escalate
- Low, medium, or high risk
- Use AI, use AI with safeguards, or do not use AI
- Verify first, review first, redact first, or ask for policy guidance

## Design Notes

Good decision exercises have more than one plausible answer. The point is the reasoning, not guessing the facilitator's preferred option.

Strong exercises include:

- enough context to reason
- one or two meaningful ambiguities
- a realistic consequence
- an opportunity to revise after new information appears
