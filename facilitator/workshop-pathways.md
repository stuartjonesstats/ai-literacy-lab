# Workshop Pathways

The curriculum should remain free and open. Workshops add facilitation, adaptation, discussion, and organizational translation.

## Self-Guided Path

Learner completes modules independently and records decisions, revisions, and reflections.

## Team Discussion Path

Small groups complete selected scenarios and compare reasoning.

Best for:

- team norms
- shared vocabulary
- surfacing uncertainty
- identifying policy gaps

## 60-Minute Orientation

Recommended structure:

- 5 minutes: framing AI literacy as judgment
- 15 minutes: Module 1 scenario
- 15 minutes: Module 2 confidence trap
- 15 minutes: risk and escalation discussion
- 10 minutes: transfer to local work

## Half-Day Applied Lab

Recommended structure:

- Module 1: capability judgment
- Module 2: confident but wrong
- Module 3: data boundaries
- Module 5: accountability and review
- Module 7: risk classification
- short capstone decision memo

## Full-Day Organizational Lab

Recommended structure:

- morning: core modules and scenarios
- midday: role-specific adaptations
- afternoon: capstone simulation and team operating principles

## Train-the-Facilitator Path

Focus areas:

- running productive struggle safely
- debriefing without lecturing
- adapting scenarios without losing the core principle
- recognizing common misconceptions
- handling policy or legal questions without overclaiming
