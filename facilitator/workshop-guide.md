# Workshop Guide

## Purpose

This guide will help facilitators run AI Literacy Lab as a discussion-rich learning experience rather than a slide presentation.

## Facilitation Stance

The facilitator's job is to create conditions for better judgment:

- ask learners to commit to an initial answer
- let ambiguity surface
- normalize revision
- connect observations to durable principles
- avoid turning every discussion into tool advice
- keep the focus on behavior, risk, evidence, and accountability

## Basic Session Pattern

1. Present a scenario.
2. Ask for individual first decisions.
3. Compare reasoning in pairs or small groups.
4. Reveal a complication.
5. Ask learners to revise.
6. Debrief the concept.
7. Transfer to local work.

## Future Workshop Formats

- 60-minute overview: one scenario plus discussion
- 90-minute lab: two modules and a mini risk classification activity
- Half-day lab: modules 1, 2, 3, 5, and 7 with a capstone
- Team adaptation session: map the framework to local policies and workflows
