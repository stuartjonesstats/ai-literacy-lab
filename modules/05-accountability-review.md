# Module 5: Human Accountability and Review

## Durable Question

Who is responsible when AI contributes to work?

## Learning Goal

Learners will distinguish AI assistance from delegated responsibility and define what meaningful human review requires.

## Signature Activity

Human-in-the-loop challenge: learners evaluate a workflow where a human technically reviews AI output but lacks the time, expertise, or authority to catch important problems.

## Productive Struggle

The scenario should expose the difference between review as a checkbox and review as a real control.

## Transfer Principle

AI can assist work, but it cannot own accountability. Human review must be capable, informed, and consequential.
