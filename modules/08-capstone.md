# Capstone: Workplace AI Decision Simulation

## Durable Question

Can the learner navigate a realistic AI decision from start to finish?

## Learning Goal

Learners will integrate capability judgment, verification, data boundaries, bias awareness, accountability, workflow design, and escalation into a defensible recommendation.

## Simulation Shape

The learner receives a realistic workplace request involving AI. Across several stages, new information appears:

1. initial request
2. proposed AI use
3. sample AI output
4. data sensitivity concern
5. affected stakeholder concern
6. accountability or review gap
7. final recommendation

## Evidence of Learning

The learner produces a short decision memo or structured recommendation that explains:

- whether to proceed
- what safeguards are needed
- what must be verified
- what data boundaries apply
- who should review or approve
- what should be documented
- what would trigger escalation

## Transfer Principle

Responsible AI use is a chain of decisions. Strong AI literacy means noticing when that chain needs evidence, safeguards, human review, or escalation.
