# Module 7: Risk Classification and Escalation

## Durable Question

Which AI uses need additional review or governance?

## Learning Goal

Learners will classify AI use cases by risk and decide when to proceed, modify, pause, document, or escalate.

## Signature Activity

Escalation fork: learners encounter ambiguous AI use cases and must defend different next steps as new information appears.

## Productive Struggle

The cases should be borderline. Learners should have to weigh consequence, reversibility, data sensitivity, affected people, verification, and policy uncertainty.

## Transfer Principle

Risk classification is contextual. The right question is not only "Can AI do this?" but "What would responsible use require here?"
