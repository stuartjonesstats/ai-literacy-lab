# Module 2: Why Confident Answers Can Be Wrong

## Durable Question

Why does fluent AI output feel more reliable than it is?

## Learning Goal

Learners will identify unsupported claims, missing evidence, false precision, and overconfident language in AI-assisted work.

## Opening Scenario

You are asked to prepare a short internal summary for a team meeting. A colleague used AI to generate a draft from a small packet of employee exit notes and says, "Looks good to me. Can you send it?"

The learner must decide whether to send, revise, reject, or ask for more data before inspecting individual claims.

## Signature Activity

Confidence trap: learners review a polished AI-generated answer that appears complete but contains subtle omissions or unsupported claims.

## Productive Struggle

The first pass should tempt learners to trust clarity, structure, and confident tone. The reveal should show that fluency is not evidence.

## Transfer Principle

Fluency is not evidence. The more consequential the use, the stronger the verification burden.

## Accuracy Note

Use a synthetic evidence packet for this module so every claim can be checked directly.
