# Internal Sources for Module 7

This file records the accuracy posture behind Module 7.

## Claim

AI risk classification should be contextual and proportional. The same broad task can require different controls depending on consequence, reversibility, data sensitivity, affected people, verification burden, policy clarity, and accountability.

## Learner Move

Classify an AI use, revise the classification when new information appears, identify the risk dimensions driving the decision, and document what would trigger escalation or allow use to proceed.

## Source Anchors

- NIST AI RMF 1.0 frames AI risk management as context-specific and governed through functions such as govern, map, measure, and manage.
  - https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-ai-rmf-10
- NIST AI RMF Generative AI Profile is a cross-sectoral companion resource for generative AI risk management.
  - https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence

## Caveat

This module does not provide a universal legal risk taxonomy and does not replace an organization's policies. It teaches a generic escalation habit using synthetic cases.

## Scenario Accuracy

The cases are synthetic. Suggested classifications are based only on supplied facts and the module's generic risk lens.
