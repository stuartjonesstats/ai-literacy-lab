# Internal Sources for Module 4

This file records the accuracy posture behind Module 4.

## Claim

Neutral-looking AI-assisted criteria can still create uneven opportunity or representational harm when they reward visibility, schedule flexibility, manager access, narrow communication norms, or unclear evidence standards.

## Learner Move

Inspect who has a realistic opportunity to satisfy criteria, identify proxy criteria, revise toward role-relevant evidence, and add review/documentation processes.

## Source Anchors

- NIST AI RMF 1.0 identifies fairness, harmful bias management, accountability, transparency, and impacts to people and organizations as part of trustworthy AI risk management.
  - https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-ai-rmf-10
- NIST AI RMF Generative AI Profile is a cross-sectoral companion resource for generative AI risk management.
  - https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence

## Caveat

This module does not provide legal advice about employment selection or discrimination. It teaches a general fairness habit using a synthetic professional development selection process.

## Scenario Accuracy

The criteria and contextual details are synthetic. Suggested revisions are based only on the supplied facts and the module's learning goal.
