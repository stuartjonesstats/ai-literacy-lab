# Internal Sources for Module 6

This file records the accuracy posture behind Module 6.

## Claim

Responsible everyday AI use is often a workflow design problem, not only a prompt-writing problem. Useful workflows define purpose, minimize data exposure, constrain generation, inspect output, verify important claims, and preserve human ownership.

## Learner Move

Identify failure modes in a one-shot prompt, choose a staged workflow, write a safer prompt or workflow instruction, and self-check for purpose, data minimization, uncertainty, verification, and final human judgment.

## Source Anchors

- NIST AI RMF 1.0 frames AI risk management around context, governance, measurement, and management.
  - https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-ai-rmf-10
- NIST Privacy Framework supports privacy risk management and privacy engineering practices.
  - https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework

## Caveat

This module does not claim that AI always improves productivity or quality. It teaches a generic workflow habit for using AI without bypassing human judgment.

## Scenario Accuracy

The briefing-note scenario is synthetic. Rubric signals are transparent local checks and should not be treated as a complete evaluation of prompt quality.
