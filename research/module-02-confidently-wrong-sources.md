# Internal Sources for Module 2

This file records the accuracy posture behind Module 2.

## Claim

AI-generated text can appear fluent, coherent, and confident while containing unsupported, overstated, or false claims.

## Learner Move

Break a polished answer into specific claims. For each important claim, ask what evidence would need to exist for the claim to be true.

## Source Anchors

- NIST AI RMF 1.0 frames trustworthy AI around characteristics including valid and reliable, accountable and transparent, explainable and interpretable, and risk management across contexts.
  - https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-ai-rmf-10
- NIST AI RMF Generative AI Profile is a cross-sectoral companion resource for generative AI risk management.
  - https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence
- Automation bias research describes overreliance on decision support and reduced vigilance in information seeking and processing.
  - https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7651899/

## Caveat

The module does not claim that AI outputs are always wrong. It claims that confidence should be earned through evidence, verification, and context rather than tone.

## Scenario Accuracy

The activity uses a synthetic evidence packet. The correct classifications are determined only from the text supplied inside the activity.
