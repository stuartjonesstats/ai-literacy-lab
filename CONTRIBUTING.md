# Contributing

AI Literacy Lab welcomes contributions that improve learning quality, accessibility, and practical usefulness.

This project is curated. It should not become a generic prompt collection, vendor showcase, or list of AI news items.

## Good Contributions

- realistic scenarios
- decision exercises
- verification challenges
- facilitator notes
- learner reflection prompts
- rubrics
- accessibility improvements
- translation and localization
- research references
- domain adaptation notes

## Scenario Contribution Checklist

When proposing a scenario or activity, include:

- the learner misconception it targets
- the productive struggle it creates
- the transferable principle it teaches
- the intended audience
- the approximate risk level
- the evidence of learning
- any assumptions or domain-specific context

## Content Standards

Contributions should:

- be generic enough to adapt across organizations
- avoid vendor-specific marketing
- avoid time-bound claims unless clearly labeled
- avoid legal, medical, financial, or employment advice
- use synthetic examples unless rights and privacy are clear
- preserve a scenario-first learning pattern
- assess reasoning rather than memorized definitions

## Tone

Write for intelligent nontechnical learners. Avoid hype, fear, jargon, and condescension.
